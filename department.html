Department of Agriculture, IUBAT

The Department of Agriculture at International University of Business Agriculture and Technology trains students in crop science, soil and environment, agricultural economics, and extension education — combining classroom study with hands-on fieldwork.

🌾 Programs

B.Sc. in Agriculture, with concentrations spanning agronomy, horticulture, soil science, and agribusiness.

🧪 Facilities

Teaching labs, a research farm, and greenhouse space used for coursework and student research projects.

🤝 Alumni network

Graduates go on to work across research institutes, agribusiness, NGOs, government extension services, and graduate study abroad.
